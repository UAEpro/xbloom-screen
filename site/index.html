How it works

Plug it in, pair once, brew forever.

01

Pair with your machine

Power it from any USB-C charger. A short setup wizard sets your PIN and finds the xBloom Studio over Bluetooth.

02

Pick a recipe

Swipe through your library. Each card shows dose, ratio, grind, temperature and every pour.

03

Load, then brew

Tap Load to send the recipe to the machine, then press start on the xBloom, or press and hold Brew on the screen.

Why you'll love it

Better coffee, less fiddling.

Loading never starts a brew.

Load only arms the machine. Water flows only when you press start on the xBloom or deliberately hold Brew on the screen, and a PIN can guard that. The phone panel can stop a brew but can never start one.
